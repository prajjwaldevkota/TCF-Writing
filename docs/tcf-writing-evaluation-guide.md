# TCF Canada writing evaluation guide

User-supplied source check: 2026-10-17. Automated recheck on 2026-11-11 was blocked by FEI (ERR-BOT-403); this is not a new verification date.

## Official public format
Expression écrite lasts 60 minutes and contains three tasks.
- Task 1: message describing, recounting and/or explaining to identified recipients; 60–120 words.
- Task 2: article, letter, note or similar recounting an experience/event, with comments, opinions or arguments serving a purpose; 120–150 words.
- Task 3: compare two viewpoints from two documents on a social issue and give a personal position; 120–180 words.
Candidates communicate clearly, provide requested information, describe/recount/explain, justify choices or positions, connect ideas coherently, express and argue opinions, and use appropriate vocabulary and structures.

## Official correction and AI limits
The supplied manual description uses two independent double-blind evaluations. Each evaluator assigns each task a level from A1 not achieved to C2; six judgments are combined into the final result, with a possible third correction for sufficiently large disagreement. The detailed combination formula and numerical domain weights are not public.
Never invent domain points, task weights, or an official aggregation formula. Never derive a final /20 or NCLC from an isolated task. A full mock-exam projection must remain broad, pedagogical and non-official. Store an official result only from an actual official result supplied by the learner.
IRCC's final TCF writing score conversion (10–11/20 to NCLC 7) applies to official ability results, not a single-task AI estimate:

## Evidence domains (not points grids)
Linguistic: vocabulary range/precision; grammatical control; spelling/punctuation; sentence construction/elaboration.
Pragmatic: task fulfilment and outcome; coherence/cohesion; progression/development; explanation, justification, comparison and argumentation where required.
Sociolinguistic: communication situation; recipient relationship; genre conventions; tone/register.

## Pedagogical two-pass method
Pass 1 — communication: requested actions, clarity, development/progression, coherence/cohesion, genre/recipient/tone/register, source use/comparison for Task 3. Read without line editing.
Pass 2 — language control: vocabulary range/precision/collocation; grammar and sentence variety; spelling/punctuation; frequency, recurrence and communicative impact of errors.
Give a holistic range such as B1–B2; explain limiting evidence; select at most five priorities. Do not average checklist entries. These are two reading passes, not independent official examiners.

## Preliminary admissibility-risk check
Flag a risk with evidence, never declare an official outcome: missing task, off-topic response, absent required actions, word-range violation, illegible handwriting (only when actually available), copied prompt/source wording, or apparently memorized/plagiarized replacement of the response. Do not accuse a learner of plagiarism without evidence. In typed text handwriting cannot be assessed.
Mechanical word count: a set of signs between spaces. Do not split punctuation, apostrophes or hyphens. Treat line breaks/other whitespace as separators in the editor.

## Task-specific checks
Task 1: immediately clear message purpose; requested details/actions; recipient-appropriate opening/tone/closing; requested description/narration/explanation.
Task 2: identifiable event/experience and progression; purposeful comments/opinions/arguments; coherent genre/readership/title where appropriate/tone/ending; concrete on-topic details.
Task 3 requires prompt AND both documents. Separately check faithful coverage of each document, explicit relationship between viewpoints, reformulation rather than copying, clear personal stance, developed explanation/consequence/example, logical progression and compatible conclusion. Without both documents, correct language but withhold the complete holistic range.

## Required feedback statements
Use the configured feedback language:
- Pedagogical level range for this task: ...
- This assessment is not official.
- One isolated task cannot determine the final /20 score or NCLC level.
When relevant add: Admissibility risk: ... with evidence.

## Learner-requested full-mock NCLC projection (optional)

Added at the learner's explicit request after the initial task-only implementation.
For a complete three-task mock with in-range responses, all instructions, and both
Task 3 documents, a separate broad NCLC writing range may be estimated holistically.
It is an uncalibrated AI readiness projection, not an official result or the product
of an official scoring formula. It must not be calculated by averaging task ranges
or stored in official-result fields. Material task-fulfilment risks may justify
withholding even with mechanically complete input. Isolated tasks and targeted
practice sessions never receive this projection.

4–5/20 → NCLC 4; 6 → 5; 7–9 → 6; 10–11 → 7; 12–13 → 8;
14–15 → 9; 16–20 → 10+. These are final official-score conversions, not a
CEFR-to-NCLC conversion or permission to invent task marks.
